# Add a news item

Create `src/content/groups/<group>/news/<topic>.md` or `src/content/shared/news/<topic>.md`.

```md
---
title: "A project update"
date: 2027-04-03
summary: "One short public sentence announcing the news."
# image: "/images/rocketry/update.jpg"
---

A photograph and a short paragraph are usually enough. Link to your group's detailed page if useful.
```

Do not publish private laboratory details, sensitive technical data, personal contact information or photos without the necessary permission.
