# Editorial and visual guidelines

- Audience: prospective students, current students, secondary-school teachers/students, partners and curious visitors.
- Each group remains independent. The shared site is a discovery gateway, not a replacement for group portals.
- Aim for 80–120 words on overview pages and fewer than 250 words in routine news posts.
- Prioritise photographs of real experiments, hardware and people (with permission), not generic clip art.
- Technical details can link to laboratory sites, research outputs, repositories and papers.
- A representative from each group reviews its entries; 1–2 editorial maintainers review shared homepage/news.
- Group logos, ISEL marks and trademarks should follow the appropriate institutional permissions.
- Never put credentials, private student data or internal-only confidential documents into a public GitHub repository.
